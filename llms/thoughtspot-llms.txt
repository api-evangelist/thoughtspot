# ThoughtSpot Documentation (docs.thoughtspot.com)

ThoughtSpot is a business intelligence and analytics platform that leverages Search and AI (Spotter) for data exploration. This documentation covers ThoughtSpot Cloud, ThoughtSpot Software (On-Premises/Private Cloud), and ThoughtSpot Mobile.

## ThoughtSpot Cloud
Documentation for the fully managed SaaS analytics platform.
- [Cloud overview](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest)
- [Release notes](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/notes) - ThoughtSpot Cloud monthly and weekly release notes.
- [Spotter Agents](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/spotter-agents) - AI-powered analytics agents.
  - [Spotter](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/spotter) - Personal AI analyst, delivering business-ready insights.
  - [SpotterModel](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/spotter-model-create) - Turn prompts into deployable data models.
  - [SpotterViz](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/spotter-viz) - Turn complex business questions into dashboards.
- [Liveboards](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/liveboard) - Live dashboards with collections of charts, tables, and headlines.
- [Answers](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/answer-experience-new) - A saved search that can be used in a Liveboard.
- [Charts](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/charts) - Classic and new charts.
- [Custom charts](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/chart-custom) - Muze Studio and VitaraCharts.
- [Collections](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/collections) - An organizational container where you can group objects to manage more easily.
- [Monitor KPIs](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/monitor) - Alerting and KPI tracking feature that notifies you when your data changes.
- [SpotIQ](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/spotiq) - Automatically analyzes your data and surfaces insights.
- [Plug-ins] (https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/thoughtspot-plugins) - Plug-ins for Google Sheets and Slides, Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, and Teams, Salesforce, and Slack
- [Data Workspace](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/data-workspace) - Managing cloud data warehouse connections and modeling.
- [Semantic layer](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/semantic-layer) - Spotter Semantics is ThoughtSpot’s governed, AI-native semantic foundation.
- [Models](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/models) - Build data models.
- [ThoughtSpot Modeling Language](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/tml) - Markup language that represents ThoughtSpot objects (Models, Answers, Liveboards, Tables, etc.) as human-readable text files — enabling version control, bulk editing, and migration of ThoughtSpot content.
- [ThoughtSpot Sync](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/thoughtspot-sync) - Push ThoughtSpot insights directly into the business apps your teams already use. 
- [Views](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/views) - A saved SQL query or search-based dataset on top of your existing data that can be used as a source for further analysis, just like a physical table.
- [Analyst Studio](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/) - Prepare, explore, and publish data for analysis in ThoughtSpot.
- [Administration](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/administration) - Everything you need to manage, secure, and configure ThoughtSpot Cloud, users, groups, orgs, data connections, system settings and customization.
- [ThoughtSpot Status](https://docs.thoughtspot.com/cloud/latest/administration) - ThoughtSpot's service page that gives you real-time visibility into the operational status of ThoughtSpot Cloud services.
